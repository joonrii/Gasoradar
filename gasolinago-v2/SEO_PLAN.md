# GasolinaGo — SEO plan

## URL hierarchy

- `/`
- `/gasolineras/`
- `/gasolineras/{provincia}/`
- `/gasolineras/{provincia}/{ciudad}/`
- `/gasolineras/{provincia}/{ciudad}/{combustible}/`
- `/gasolinera/{slug}/`

## Indexation principles

1. Index only pages with useful, non-empty data.
2. Every indexable page needs unique title, description and visible local data.
3. Add canonical URLs.
4. Generate sitemap automatically from actual pages.
5. Use breadcrumbs and contextual internal links.
6. Do not create thin combinations for every possible filter.

## First launch

Prioritize:

- Spain hub.
- All provinces with enough stations.
- Main cities with meaningful search demand/data.
- Individual station pages only when their data is complete and useful.

## Primary intent

The product should target searches such as:

- gasolineras baratas en {ciudad}
- precio gasolina {ciudad}
- precio gasolina hoy {ciudad}
- gasolineras cerca de mí
- gasolinera más barata {ciudad}

SEO landing pages must always lead into the product: location, map, best option and directions.
